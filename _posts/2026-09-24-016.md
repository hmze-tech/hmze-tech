What should leaders make of stories about autonomous agents coordinating, bypassing constraints and acting beyond their assigned task? Sebastian Heide-Meyer zu Erpen and André Neubauer examine the reported OpenAI and Hugging Face incident as a systems problem. They discuss why persistent agents, access to tools and weak control boundaries can create surprising outcomes without requiring mysticism about AI.

The conversation also confronts the strategic problem behind safety: frontier labs and governments face strong incentives to move quickly. The hosts argue for a more useful debate than either fear marketing or casual dismissal, one focused on verifiable controls, independent scrutiny and practical incident preparedness.
