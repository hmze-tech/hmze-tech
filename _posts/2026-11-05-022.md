What does AI transformation look like when the starting point is not a pilot but the future viability of the business? Thomas Wollermann of Merantix Momentum explains why AI strategy has become business strategy. The conversation moves from board-level questions about defensibility and market change into the practical work of deploying systems, changing processes and helping people remain in control.

Thomas shares a concrete model for reliable AI systems: combine probabilistic methods such as evaluation and fine-tuning with hard, deterministic checks. He connects agent design to Team Topologies, Domain-Driven Design and clear interfaces, then explains why AI sovereignty is less about owning every layer than retaining meaningful choices around hardware, models, data and trusted partners.

The episode also looks at where impact appears first. Knowledge retrieval is an obvious cross-industry use case, while scenario modelling can support better decisions on pricing, markets and operations. Financial ROI is valuable when it is observable, but transformation teams should also watch whether the operating engine is improving: cycle time, incidents, decision speed, reversals and quality.
