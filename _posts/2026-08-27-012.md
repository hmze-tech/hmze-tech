How should engineering leaders respond when AI progress feels both faster than expected and impossible to predict? Raz Shuty, Group CTO at auxmoney, joins Sebastian Heide-Meyer zu Erpen and André Neubauer to unpack Amara's Law in the context of AI. Their conclusion is neither complacent nor apocalyptic: the role of the engineer is changing, while the need for people who can reason about systems, specifications, and risk remains.

Raz explains how a regulated fintech can make room for tool choice without sacrificing safety. The conversation covers secure enterprise AI access, education, rigorous CI/CD and review practices, and the difference between a rapid POC and a product that belongs in production. It also explores why AI can increase an organisation's capacity to pursue ideas rather than simply shrink teams.

The episode closes on a more human implication. If AI makes building more accessible, leaders need to help teams adapt, without scaring new engineers away from the profession just as its responsibilities become more consequential.
