Markus Andrezak returns to Beyond Vibe Coding to explain what changed after the first wave of AI tinkering. His answer is not a new prompt recipe. It is a practical move from chat windows to repeatable agentic systems: static context, selected live information, clear outcomes and routines that produce useful artifacts.

For product leaders, the conversation lands on a harder question. If a PRD or strategy contains assumptions, can it show the interviews, quotes and patterns that support them? Markus argues that agents can help make product reasoning traceable. That does not remove human responsibility. It makes the final judgement easier to challenge with evidence.

The episode also covers stakeholder mapping, synthetic research, the right level of interaction with agentic systems, local-model realism and why deterministic guardrails are becoming an essential part of reliable AI work.
