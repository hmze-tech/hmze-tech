Tomas Vocetka, CTO of Omio, joins Beyond Vibe Coding to describe what happens when agentic engineering becomes an operating model rather than a side experiment. At Omio, agents help with complex partner integrations, production diagnosis and day-to-day software delivery. The aim is not to optimize for code volume. It is to improve real product outcomes while engineers supervise, validate and release agent-produced work.

The episode’s central idea is Omio’s monthly-mission model: one engineer, one mission, one month. Product defines the outcome and the success measures. Engineers own delivery with agents and a supporting harness of repository guidance, tests and organizational knowledge. Teams continue to own systems, but the boundary around who can work where becomes more fluid.

We also discuss why design and product become more important as implementation accelerates, why prototypes can replace part of the specification process and why managing agents increasingly resembles managing people: goals, delegation, feedback and judgment still matter.
