Agents are changing the economics of cybersecurity. Christian Matthies, founder of CISOIQ, joins Sebastian and André to explain why finding vulnerabilities is becoming cheaper while remediation remains the harder and more expensive outcome. They discuss how defenders can keep pace with an expanding, constantly changing attack surface.

The conversation covers agent-assisted security workflows, reducing internet exposure, citizen-built applications and the limits of asset visibility. Christian also shares his perspective on phishing-resistant authentication, credential blast radius and the future of AI-generated code, while the hosts challenge his assumptions about dependencies and software security.
